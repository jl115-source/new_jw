Weather is a physical system.
Markets are a human one.

I work at the intersection of weather, energy and quantitative research — using physical understanding, data and computation to make uncertain systems more useful for decisions.

ABOUT

Quantitative work grounded in the atmosphere.

My background spans atmospheric science, geophysics, machine learning and energy. I am interested in problems where the forecast is only the beginning: how uncertainty propagates into demand, renewables, market fundamentals and risk.

I like building systems that combine physical reasoning with statistical models, forecast verification and robust software — especially when the output has to be understood quickly by people making real decisions.
